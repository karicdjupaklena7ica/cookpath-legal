Privacy

Last updated 30 September 2026

What stays on your phone

Your answers (training, allergies, foods you don't eat, time, who you cook for), your plan and shopping list, your daily goals, plates and their photos, recipes you add, saved dishes and badges. Without an account, nothing leaves your phone.

What goes online when you sign in

  • Your email address (or your Apple or Google sign-in), to sign you in.
  • A copy of the things above, so they come back on another phone: including your allergies, the foods you skip, your goals and your week plan.
  • Plate photos, made smaller first.

It is stored with Supabase in the EU (Ireland). Only you can read it.

Your body details stay on the phone

The goals calculator asks for age, sex, height and weight to work out a starting number. Those are kept on this phone only and are never sent online.

Allergies

Your allergies and the foods you skip are only used to hide dishes or suggest swaps. We don't share them with anyone.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics. We never sell or share your data.

Camera and photos

Used only when you take or pick a plate photo. We don't look at your other photos. Saving a share card puts one picture in your gallery when you tap Save.

Videos

Dish clips are streamed from our storage the first time you play one and kept on your phone afterwards. That request carries no account or personal information.

Delete it all

Settings → Delete account removes your account and everything in it, online and on this phone, at once. Signing out keeps everything on the phone.

Purchases

Paid by Google or Apple; we never see your card. A purchase service (RevenueCat) tells the app whether you have Plus, using an anonymous app id.
