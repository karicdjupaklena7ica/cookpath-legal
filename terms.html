What Cookpath is

An app to plan your week of real dishes, shop for it and cook it, with a link to the video of the cook who inspired each dish, your own plates and your own goals. Using it means you agree to these terms.

Cook safely

Knives, heat and raw food can hurt you. Follow the steps with care. If you have an allergy, check every label yourself: Cookpath hides dishes by your answers and may suggest swaps, but it can't know every ingredient in what you buy.

Your numbers

Calories and protein are the goals you choose, and the calculator is only an estimate for adults. The calories and macros of recipes are worked out from USDA food data and the amounts written in the recipe, so they are estimates: for a recipe you write yourself they can be off by 10–15 % or more, and a line the app could not read is not counted. Cookpath does not give medical or diet advice; talk to a professional about your health.
